Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

This site uses a small consent preference stored in your browser, plus optional analytics cookies if you accept non-essential cookies. Nothing loads until you choose.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. It lets the site remember information between page loads.

Cookies used on this site

Name Type Purpose Duration
isaura-cookie-consent Essential Stores whether you accepted or denied non-essential cookies so the banner respects your choice across the site. 12 months
Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*) Analytics Helps me understand how visitors use the site (page views, general usage patterns). Only set if you accept non-essential cookies. Up to 2 years

The consent preference is stored in your browser's local storage. Google Analytics is provided by Google and only loads after you opt in.

Managing cookies

Use the banner to accept or deny non-essential cookies. Denying keeps everything limited to the essential consent preference. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Removing the consent preference will make the banner appear again so you can choose once more.

Third-party services

Links to Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, YouTube, LinkedIn, or TidyCal may set their own cookies once you visit their platforms. Please read their policies for details, because my banner and consent storage only cover this site.
