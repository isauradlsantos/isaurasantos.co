✻sanity 02

03  /  What it does

The whole thing, one page

  1. 01 Asks what you’re there to do before LinkedIn loads.
  2. 02 Six intents: message, post, research, inbox, notifications, scroll & comment.
  3. 03 A timer you set, 5 to 25 minutes.
  4. 04 Countdown on the Chrome badge and in the popup.
  5. 05 A soft bell at the end, then LinkedIn locks.
  6. 06 Closing the tab early ends the session and logs how long you stayed.
  7. 07 Daily limits on minutes and sessions, reset each local day.
  8. 08 A one-hour pause, for when you really need it.

11  /  Install

Four steps, one minute

  1. 01Save the sanity folder somewhere on your computer.
  2. 02Open chrome://extensions/.
  3. 03Turn on Developer mode.
  4. 04Click Load unpacked and select the sanity folder.
