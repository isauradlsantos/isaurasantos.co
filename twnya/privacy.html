Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

TWNYA — Creative Mentorship by Isaura Santos

This website is operated by Isaura Santos.

TWNYA (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or join TWNYA.

What We Collect

  • Booking and membership information: name, email, and any details you provide when scheduling sessions or joining TWNYA via TidyCal.
  • Communication information: messages or information you share when contacting us by email or other communication channels.
  • Analytics data: information about how visitors use the website (via Google Analytics), such as page views, browser type, device type, and approximate location.

How We Use Your Information

  • To manage TWNYA memberships and schedule mentorship sessions.
  • To communicate with you regarding your participation in TWNYA.
  • To understand how visitors use the website and improve the experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Who We Share With

  • TidyCal – to manage bookings and memberships.
  • Stripe – to process payments securely through TidyCal.
  • Google Analytics – to collect anonymized website usage data.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

  • Membership and booking information: kept as long as necessary to manage your participation in TWNYA and related communication.
  • Analytics data: retained for up to 26 months (Google Analytics default).

Your Rights (EU GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request portability of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us using the email below.

US Privacy Rights

If you are a US resident, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws (such as California CPRA). These may include the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

TWNYA does not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.
